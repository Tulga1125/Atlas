SECTION 1



Executive summary and product
definition
Atlas Charts should become the trusted explanation layer for Mongolia's music market, not
merely a copied ranking page.

Atlas Charts is Mongolia's proposed music-intelligence and editorial infrastructure.
It collects chart and performance data, preserves historical truth, identifies
meaningful change, explains why the change matters, and helps an editorial team
turn verified evidence into timely news and analysis.

1.1 The two connected products
    Product                             Purpose                                    Primary users

    Atlas Dashboard                     Private operating system for collection    Data editors, analysts, writers, editors,
                                        health, chart analysis, identity           administrators
                                        management, anomalies, story leads,
                                        assignments, and exports.

    Atlas Charts public platform        Public chart authority containing          Music audience, artists, labels, media,
                                        current rankings, histories, artist/song   industry partners
                                        pages, methodology, news, and
                                        shareable chart products.




1.2 Core product promise
    The daily question Atlas must answer
    What changed in Mongolia's music market, how unusual is it, what evidence supports it, and which story
    should the editorial team publish?


1.3 Strategic starting point
      Begin with Apple Music Top 100: Mongolia as the single automated chart source.
      Collect and permanently store one validated snapshot every day.
      Build reliable song and artist identity resolution before adding more sources.
      Create the internal newsroom dashboard before investing heavily in the public website.
      Add YouTube second, Spotify third, and an Atlas composite methodology only after source histories are
       trustworthy.

1.4 Long-term position
The long-term ambition is for Atlas Charts to play a Mongolia-specific role comparable in function - though not
necessarily scale - to respected national chart and music-intelligence institutions. The defensible value is not
access to a list; it is the combination of historical data, local identity knowledge, transparent methodology,
anomaly detection, and editorial interpretation.

                                                         and collection health.

                                                         may be included in late MVP.




2.2 Explicit non-goals for the first release
      Do not build a fully weighted official multi-platform Atlas chart in the first release.
      Do not treat public Spotify or YouTube values as complete national consumption measurements without
       documented provenance.
      Do not allow AI-generated explanations to publish without a human verifying the evidence.
      Do not permit editors to silently alter source chart positions.
      Do not overbuild machine learning before enough historical data exists.
      Do not turn the dashboard into a generic project-management or financial tracker.
      Do not launch many chart categories before the core daily chart is operationally reliable.

SECTION 3



Users, roles, and primary workflows
Permissions and interfaces should match actual newsroom and data operations.

3.1 User roles
    Role                                                    Permissions and responsibilities

    Administrator                                           Full access; manage users, sources, job reruns, entity
                                                            merges/splits, and methodology configuration.

    Data editor                                             Review matches, fix metadata, resolve duplicate entities,
                                                            review collection issues, and verify source mappings.

    Editor                                                  Review signals, prioritize and assign stories, approve drafts,
                                                            and manage publication.

    Writer                                                  Open evidence-backed leads, write drafts, submit revisions,
                                                            and view relevant chart context.

    Analyst / viewer                                        Read dashboards and export permitted reports without
                                                            altering canonical data.




3.2 Morning editorial workflow
1. Open the Command Center and confirm that the latest Apple Music snapshot is complete.
2. Review the new No. 1, biggest movers, new entries, re-entries, local artist performance, and active
   anomalies.
3. Open the generated Daily Atlas Brief and inspect the evidence behind each recommended story.
4. Confirm, dismiss, or reclassify each signal. Data issues are routed to the data editor.
5. Assign verified story leads to writers with a suggested angle and deadline.
6. Approve final copy and attach chart graphics or exports.
7. Record publication links so Atlas can connect stories to the underlying chart event.

3.3 Data editor workflow
1. Review unmatched or low-confidence platform assets.
2. Create a new canonical song/recording or link the asset to an existing entity.
3. Resolve artist aliases and Cyrillic/Latin variants.
4. Merge duplicates only after reviewing all connected history and credits.
5. Split incorrect merges without losing historical source evidence.
6. Review incomplete snapshots, stale payloads, unusual ID changes, and collector errors.

3.4 Public visitor workflow
      See the current chart and daily/weekly movement.
      Open a song or artist to view chart history, peak, tenure, and milestones.
      Read Atlas coverage explaining important changes.
      Browse chart archives and methodology.
      Share chart cards or links.

5.1 Apple Music MVP collector
    MVP requirement
    Collect the Apple Music Top 100 songs chart for the Mongolia storefront (country/storefront code MN where
    supported by the selected source). The collector must use a stable, permitted source and retain enough
    evidence to reproduce what Atlas received.

    Field                                                   Purpose

    job_id                                                  Unique collection attempt identifier.

    chart_id                                                Atlas chart definition being collected.

    scheduled_for                                           Expected collection time in Asia/Ulaanbaatar.

    started_at / completed_at                               Actual execution timestamps in UTC.

    status                                                  queued, running, succeeded, failed, partial, rejected.

    attempt_number                                          Retry counter.

                                                            reference.

    checksum                                                Hash for duplicate/stale payload detection.

    error_code / message                                    Structured error and human-readable detail.




5.2 Snapshot validation rules
    Rule                                                    Behavior

    Entry count                                             Expected 100 unique ranked entries unless the source
                                                            explicitly returns a documented exception.

    Rank sequence                                           Ranks must be integers, unique, and normally span 1
                                                            through 100.

    Item identity                                           Every row must contain a stable source item identifier or be
                                                            rejected for manual investigation.

    Timestamp freshness                                     Payload must not be older than the allowed freshness
                                                            window.

    Duplicate detection                                     Checksum and item sequence are compared with recent
                                                            snapshots to identify stale collection.

    Required metadata                                       At minimum: rank, source item ID, title, credited artist,
                                                            source URL or retrievable ID.

    Cross-row validity                                      No duplicate ranks; duplicate source IDs are flagged.

    Source schema                                           Unexpected schema changes fail safely and preserve the
                                                            raw response.




5.3 Idempotency and retries
      A rerun for the same chart and snapshot date must not create duplicate approved snapshots.
      Each attempt is logged separately; one attempt may be promoted as the approved snapshot.
      Retries are bounded and use increasing delays. Repeated failure creates a high-severity operational alert.
      Calculations run only after a snapshot is approved and committed.
      If a corrected source payload is collected later, Atlas stores a revision relationship and an audit record
       rather than silently replacing evidence.

6.2 Matching priority
1. Exact ISRC match when available and trustworthy.
2. Existing platform asset ID already linked to a recording.
3. Normalized title plus normalized credited artists.
4. Duration similarity within configurable tolerance.
5. Release date and album consistency.
6. Label, distributor, and credit metadata.
7. Fuzzy text and transliteration match.
8. Manual review when confidence is below the automatic threshold.

6.3 Normalization rules
      Normalize whitespace, capitalization, punctuation, apostrophes, and Unicode equivalents.
      Parse common featuring markers such as feat., ft., featuring, and with.
      Remove non-title platform suffixes such as Official Video only when the asset type confirms the suffix is
       descriptive.
      Preserve meaningful version markers such as Remix, Live, Acoustic, Remastered, Clean, or Sped Up.
      Maintain Cyrillic, Latin, and alternative spellings as aliases rather than overwriting the canonical display
       name.
      Treat artist order as potentially meaningful but support unordered comparison for match suggestions.
      Keep the original platform strings unchanged for evidence and debugging.

6.4 Match confidence and review
    Confidence                                                Suggested behavior

    0.95-1.00                                                 Automatic link allowed when no contradictory evidence
                                                              exists.

    0.80-0.94                                                 Auto-suggest; require review for first-time song mapping or
                                                              conflicting metadata.

    0.50-0.79                                                 Manual review required.

    Below 0.50                                                Create a new entity candidate or request additional
                                                              metadata.



    Open decision
    Final thresholds should be tuned after reviewing real Mongolian catalog examples. Automatic linking must
    prioritize avoiding false merges over reducing manual work.


6.5 Merge and split safety
      Merge operations require a preview of affected chart entries, metrics, credits, stories, and aliases.
      The old IDs remain addressable through redirect or merge history records.
      Every merge records actor, timestamp, reason, before/after IDs, and affected row counts.
      Split operations restore specific assets or recordings without deleting historical source entries.
      Undo must be possible for recent administrative mistakes.

SECTION 8



Chart calculations, metrics, and ranking
methodology
Calculations must be deterministic, versioned, and traceable to exact snapshots.

8.1 Required per-entry calculations
 Metric                                                  Definition

 current_rank                                            Rank in the selected snapshot.

 previous_rank                                           Rank in the most recent prior approved snapshot; null if absent.

 daily_rank_change                                       previous_rank - current_rank. Positive means rising.

 rank_7d_ago                                             Rank from the closest approved snapshot seven business dates
                                                         earlier.

 weekly_rank_change                                      rank_7d_ago - current_rank.

 entry_status                                            new, re-entry, up, down, unchanged, or missing/exit context.

 peak_rank                                               Best rank achieved through the selected date.

 days_on_chart                                           Count of approved daily snapshots containing the song.

 consecutive_days                                        Current uninterrupted run of appearances.

 weeks_on_chart                                          Number of distinct Atlas chart weeks containing the song.

 top_10_days / top_40_days                               Count of appearances within thresholds.

 average_rank                                            Average rank across relevant history or selected window.

 rolling_7d_average_rank                                 Mean rank across recent approved appearances, with missing-
                                                         day policy documented.

 velocity                                                Rate of rank change over a defined window.

 acceleration                                            Change in velocity between consecutive windows.

 volatility                                              Degree of rank variation over a defined period.

 momentum_score                                          Configurable 0-100 editorial prioritization indicator.




8.2 Entry status logic
IF song not present in any earlier approved snapshot:
    entry_status = "new"
ELSE IF song absent from immediately prior snapshot:
    entry_status = "re-entry"
ELSE IF previous_rank > current_rank:
    entry_status = "up"
ELSE IF previous_rank < current_rank:
    entry_status = "down"
ELSE:
    entry_status = "unchanged"



8.3 Suggested momentum score v1
Momentum is an editorial sorting aid, not an official chart position. The formula should be configurable and
versioned. A recommended first model is:




                                                                                                       ATLAS CHARTS | 16
ATLAS CHARTS / PRODUCT AND TECHNICAL SPECIFICATION



    position_importance = (101 - current_rank) / 100
    positive_daily     = normalize(max(daily_rank_change, 0), chart context)
    positive_weekly    = normalize(max(weekly_rank_change, 0), chart context)
    streak_component = normalize(consecutive_gain_days)
    peak_component    = 1 when new career peak, else 0
    local_component   = configurable editorial boost, not a chart ranking change

    momentum_score = 100 * clamp(
        0.30 * positive_daily
      + 0.25 * positive_weekly
      + 0.20 * position_importance
      + 0.15 * streak_component
      + 0.10 * peak_component,
      0, 1
    )

    Important distinction
    A song moving from No. 90 to No. 60 gained 30 places, but a move from No. 4 to No. 2 may be more
    important editorially. Movement size must be interpreted alongside current position, duration, prior
    baseline, and milestone significance.


8.4 Artist-level aggregation
     Number of charting songs today and within the selected period.
     Best current rank and best historical peak.
     Combined position points, using a transparent position-points function.
     Top 10 and Top 40 appearances.
     New entries, re-entries, and new peaks by the artist.
     Combined artist momentum based on song-level values without double-counting duplicate versions.
     Share of the chart by number of entries and optional position-weighted points.

8.5 Composite Atlas chart - later phase
    Later-phase concept
    A previously discussed starting framework was audio streaming 40%, YouTube 30%, social signals 10%,
    radio 10%, digital downloads 5%, and verified fan voting 5%. This is not an approved final methodology. It
    requires source quality evaluation, fraud controls, market research, public documentation, and versioned
    governance.

SECTION 9



Trend, signal, anomaly, and data-quality
engine
The system should separate publishable market events from operational data problems.

9.1 Signal categories
Signal                                               Meaning

New No. 1                                            The chart leader changes or a song reaches No. 1 for the first
                                                     time.

Highest new entry                                    Best-ranked first-time entry in the snapshot.

Fastest climber                                      Largest meaningful rise adjusted for position and context.

Breakout                                             Rapid multi-day rise beyond the song or comparable cohort
                                                     baseline.

Slow burn                                            Sustained gradual improvement across a longer period.

Sudden revival                                       Older or previously declining song returns or rises unexpectedly.

New career peak                                      Song reaches its best rank to date.

Threshold crossing                                   First Top 50, Top 40, Top 20, Top 10, or No. 1.

Artist takeover                                      Multiple songs by one artist enter or dominate simultaneously.

Catalog effect                                       Older songs rise alongside a new release or artist event.

Local breakout                                       Mongolian artist significantly exceeds prior baseline.

International release wave                           Several tracks from one international project enter or rise
                                                     together.

Resilience                                           Song remains unusually stable while peers decline.

High volatility                                      Repeated movement outside a normal range.




9.2 Data-quality anomaly categories
Anomaly                                              Example

Incomplete snapshot                                  Fewer than expected valid entries.

Stale payload                                        Same checksum or source timestamp outside an allowed
                                                     window.

Rank structure error                                 Missing, duplicate, or invalid ranks.

Duplicate identity                                   Same source asset or likely same song appears more than
                                                     expected.

Unexpected metadata mutation                         Title, artist credit, artwork, or IDs change sharply.

Mass chart replacement                               Unusually large share of entries changes, potentially indicating a
                                                     source or parsing issue.

Impossible metric movement                           View/stream counters decrease or change beyond plausible
                                                     bounds.

Collector schema change                              Source fields or format change unexpectedly.

Entity-link conflict                                 Incoming asset strongly matches multiple canonical recordings.

9.3 Rule engine v1
    Rule ID                              Condition                                  Default severity

    RANK_JUMP_20                         Daily rise of 20 or more places.           medium; increase near Top 20

    NEW_TOP_10                           First recorded appearance inside Top 10.   high

9.4 Statistical detection later
      Rolling 7-day and 30-day baselines.
      Median absolute deviation for robust outlier detection.
      Percentile thresholds by chart zone, artist history, and release age.
      Change-point detection for sustained trend shifts.
      Expected-rank bands and cohort comparison.
      Cross-platform confirmation once YouTube and Spotify histories exist.

9.5 Signal evidence model
      Every signal stores the rule or model version that created it.
      Evidence rows link to exact snapshots, chart entries, metrics, and comparison windows.
      A human-readable explanation is generated from stored facts, not from ungrounded AI inference.
      Editors can confirm, dismiss, convert to a lead, or classify as a data issue.
      Signals remain in history even after dismissal so rule quality can be measured.

14.1 Authentication and authorization
     Require authenticated accounts for all internal routes.

14.2 Audit events
     Entity create, merge, split, redirect, and metadata correction.
     Snapshot approval, rejection, replacement, and reprocessing.
     Anomaly review and dismissal.
     Story assignment, status transition, approval, and publication record changes.
     Methodology and calculation version changes.
     Role, permission, and source configuration changes.
     Manual imports and exports.

14.3 Data provenance
    Required provenance question
    For any number shown by Atlas, the system must be able to answer: Where did it come from, when was it
    collected, how was it calculated, which version produced it, and has a person changed any related
    metadata?


14.4 Methodology governance
     Store methodology versions with effective dates and explanatory notes.
     Historical outputs retain the version used at the time or can be explicitly recomputed under a new version.
     Editorial users cannot alter source ranks or official calculation weights.
     Any manual exclusion or exception requires a reason, approver, scope, and visible audit record.

14.5 Secrets and source credentials
     Store credentials in managed environment secrets, never in client bundles or source control.
     Rotate keys and restrict each secret to the minimum source and environment needed.
     Redact credentials, tokens, and private payload data from application logs.
