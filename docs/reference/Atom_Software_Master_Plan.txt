Mongolian Phonographic Industry Association — structuring the body that applies to IFPI for

Why the Association Must Exist
IFPI designates national phonographic associations — not individual companies — as national
ISRC agencies. A single company applying looks like a private monopoly. An industry
association with multiple founding members looks like the recording industry speaking with one
voice. The distinction is everything for IFPI's approval process.


  The most important design decision: the charter must include a non-exclusivity clause. ISRC codes
  must be available to all qualifying Mongolian entities regardless of membership status. The moat is
  not controlling who can get codes — it's that Atlas is the permanent keeper of the registry database.

ISRC Code Anatomy
The International Standard Recording Code (ISO 3901) is a 12-character identifier permanently
assigned to each unique sound recording. It is the global standard used by every DSP, radio
monitor, and sync licensing system in the world.



                     MN         -        ATL           -      25       -       00001
                    COUNTRY           REGISTRANT              YEAR            DESIGNATION

                                       by

 Country code       2 chars            IFPI                Always MN for this registry. Locked to the IFPI-
 (MN)                                                      designated national agency.

 Registrant code    3                  Atlas / MPIA        Assigned to each label, distributor, or artist. This is
 (ATL)              alphanumeric                           what Atlas controls. Max 46,656 unique codes.

 Year of            2 digits           Registrant          Year ISRC was issued. Validated ±1 year of current
 reference (25)                                            date. Not the release year.

 Designation        5 digits           Registrant          Sequential 00001–99999 per registrant per year.
 (00001)                               (auto)              Auto-incremented atomically by registry.

Audit Chain Integrity

 -- Postgres trigger: compute hash chain on every audit_log INSERT
 CREATE OR REPLACE FUNCTION compute_audit_checksum() RETURNS TRIGGER AS $$
 DECLARE prev_hash CHAR(64);




                                                          15
  BEGIN
    SELECT checksum INTO prev_hash FROM audit_log
    WHERE seq = (SELECT MAX(seq) FROM audit_log);
    NEW.prev_checksum := prev_hash;
    NEW.checksum := encode(sha256((
        NEW.seq::text || NEW.event_type || NEW.entity_id::text
        || NEW.payload::text || COALESCE(prev_hash, '')
    )::bytea), 'hex');
    RETURN NEW;
  END;
  $$ LANGUAGE plpgsql;


  -- Auditor verification: returns 0 rows if chain is clean
  SELECT seq, event_type, created_at,
    checksum = encode(sha256((
        seq::text || event_type || entity_id::text
        || payload::text || COALESCE(prev.checksum, '')
    )::bytea), 'hex') AS chain_intact
  FROM audit_log a LEFT JOIN audit_log prev ON prev.seq = a.seq - 1
  WHERE chain_intact = false;

Fraud Detection Engine

   IFPI requirement: fraud detection methodology must be documented and demonstrably applied
   before each chart publication. All flagged and removed streams must be logged to the audit trail
   with the triggering rule and evidence payload.



   HARD REMOVE             Velocity anomaly — single-day spike
                           A track receives >800% of its trailing 28-day daily average in a single 24-hour window. All
                           streams for that track on that day are quarantined. Requires manual admin review to
                           release. Threshold: day_count > avg_28d × 8.0




                                                             20
 HARD REMOVE          Geographic IP clustering
                      More than 65% of streams in a 24-hour window originate from the same /24 IP subnet.
                      Streams from that subnet removed for that day. Applied per-DSP where IP data is available
                      (YouTube primarily).


 HARD REMOVE          New account concentration
                      More than 25% of streams come from accounts created within the past 7 days. New
                      account streams removed entirely. Applied per-DSP where account age data is available
                      (Spotify partner data).


 SOFT REDUCE      Abnormal skip rate
                  Skip rate falls below 8% (real listeners skip ~25–35%). Streams reduced by
                  credibility_factor = max(skip_rate / 0.25, 0.30). Applied where skip data available (Spotify).


 SOFT REDUCE      Repeat stream pattern
                  Device/cookie fingerprint streams the same track more than 12 times in a 3-hour window.
                  Streams beyond the 3rd play per hour from a single device are excluded.


 SOFT REDUCE      Algorithmic cadence detection
                  Streams arrive at statistically regular intervals (coefficient of variation of inter-stream gap <
                  0.05 over 100+ samples). Indicates bot-driven playback. Streams from matching accounts
                  reduced 80%.


 MONITOR       Unusual listener-to-stream ratio
               Fewer than 0.3 unique listeners per 100 streams (normal: 1.5–3.0). Flagged for human review
               — may be legitimate (event/venue). Admin can approve or remove.


 MONITOR       Cross-DSP inconsistency
               Track appears in top 10 on one DSP but is absent on all others in the same week. Flagged for
               review — may indicate organic DSP-specific virality or manipulation. No automatic removal.
